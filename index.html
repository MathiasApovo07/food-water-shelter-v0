Info / feedback

Press Start. Each lizard has eight seconds to find its resource.

A successful match replaces one lizard with two. An unmet need causes death and returns one resource.

Read the habitat

Food, water and shelter limit the population. Carrying capacity is the population a habitat can support. Observe whether the graph settles, and how shortages change it. Your matching pace also affects the result.

Keyboard: Tab to a lizard, then Enter or Space. Select a matching resource the same way. Escape cancels selection. On touchscreens, drag from a lizard or use two taps; scroll from outside a lizard.
