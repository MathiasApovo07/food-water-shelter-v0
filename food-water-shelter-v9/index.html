Food, Water, Shelter: Explore the Sagebrush Steppe

Within an ecosystem, an organism's habitat includes food, water, and shelter. However, these resources are limited, which limits the size of the population that can exist within that ecosystem. This limited population size is referred to as the carrying capacity.

This game represents a simplified version of a sagebrush steppe ecosystem. You will play as sagebrush lizards, which eat insects and use sagebrush as shelter.

Click to match the sagebrush lizards to the resources they need before the time runs out!

Lizards who get what they need will live long enough to reproduce. If the lizard doesn't get to the resource in time, it will die, feeding back into the habitat through decomposition.

A wildfire sweeps through the ecosystem, burning some of the sagebrush.

The Sagebrush Steppe is a fire-adapted ecosystem, which means that wildfires naturally occur periodically. The frequency that wildfires tend to occur is referred to as an ecosystem's fire regime. Wildfire has an important role in an ecosystem, removing dead plant matter and allowing for new growth. While wildfires may appear to momentarily harm an ecosystem, they are an important abiotic factor in allowing an ecosystem to thrive in the long-term.
